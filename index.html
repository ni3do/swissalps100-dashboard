MVP data

Execution doctrine

First third: boring restraint.

Hike early on steep grades, keep HR controlled, no “free speed” debt. The race starts properly after Reckingen.

Middle: fuel like a machine.

Stay on the clock: 30–35g carbs every 20 min, sodium steady, no late improvised solids. Tiny planned bites only while gut is calm.

Final third: disciplined aggression.

From Grengiols/Mühlebach: commit to forward pressure. Run what is runnable, hike with intent, crew keeps transitions sharp.

Gut rescue protocol.

If cramps start: 10–15 min easier, small sips water, skip one carb dose, restart at 20–25g every 20 min.

18h math

Average elapsed pace required:

10:43 / km

18h target uses 66.7% of the 27h cutoff. Plenty of buffer — if transitions are ruthless.

Carbs

90–100g/h

Default: split into 30–35g every 20 minutes. Do not chase hunger with random solids after 2h.

Sodium

600–900mg/h

Adjust upward if hot/sweaty. Keep a separate sodium source so carbs and salt can be controlled independently.

Fluid

500–750ml/h

Weather-adjusted. Avoid overdrinking; steady sips beat heroic chugging at aid stations.

Crew jobs

Before arrival

Bottle ready, gels counted, sodium ready, headlamp/night gear if needed, quick status question only.

At arrival

Swap bottles first. Ask: stomach? feet? cold/hot? Then solve one problem. Keep moving.

Departure script

“Next section is X km. Eat at :20/:40/:00. You are on schedule. Go.”

Pacer handoff

Official page says pacers from Reckingen to finish. MVP rule: pacer is a metronome, not a therapist.

Primary job

Keep fueling cadence and hiking pressure alive.

Forbidden

Long negotiations at aid stations. Wandering pace. Random food experiments.

On body

Race shoes tested, socks, vest, soft flasks, poles if using, phone, watch, emergency layer, basic first aid.

Drop bag: Binn

Spare socks, backup gels, sodium, anti-chafe, warm layer, headlamp/battery logic depending on arrival and weather.

Finish / backup

Dry clothes, recovery drink/food, warm jacket, sandals, charger, medical/feet kit.
