20h math

Average elapsed pace required:

11:54 / km

20h target uses 74.1% of the 27h cutoff. Plenty of buffer — if transitions are ruthless.

Carbs

90–100g/h

30–35g every 20 minutes: gel/chews at :20 and :40, something solid at the top of every hour. No improvised extras.

Sodium

600–900mg/h

Adjust upward if hot/sweaty. Keep a separate sodium source so carbs and salt can be controlled independently.

Fluid

500–750ml/h

Weather-adjusted. Avoid overdrinking; steady sips beat heroic chugging at aid stations.
