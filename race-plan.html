Historic pre-race plan

Fueling targets.

Carbohydrates90–100g / h

Planned as approximately 30–35g every 20 minutes: gel or chews around :20 and :40, something solid at the hour.

Sodium600–900mg / h

Planned to rise with heat and sweat rate, carried separately so salt and carbohydrate could be adjusted independently.

Fluid500–750ml / h

Weather-dependent planned range, favouring regular sips over large intake at aid stations.

Historic crew & pacer plan

Four hand-offs after Blitzingen.

Carmen · Blitzingen → Chäserstatt

First planned pacer leg. Blitzingen was a crew-accessible meeting point; the plan then continued up the mountain road to Chäserstatt.

Andrea · Chäserstatt → Binn

Second planned pacer leg, from the high mountain station through the Binntal side of the course.

Jodi · Binn → Mühlebach

Third planned pacer leg, covering Breithorn and Grengiols before the Mühlebach changeover.

Matthias · Mühlebach → Finish Fiesch

Final planned pacer leg into Fiesch, with the crew intended to be at the race centre ahead of the projected arrival.

Pacer assignments and crew travel notes were planning aids only. They do not assert who accompanied which completed segment.

Historic required kit

Mandatory equipment list.

  • Backpack or waist belt
  • Mobile phone, switched on
  • Emergency blanket
  • Whistle
  • Elastic emergency bandage
  • Waterproof, breathable jacket
  • Long tights or leg coverings
  • Gloves
  • Water bottle or hydration bladder
  • Headlamp and spare batteries

Preserved from the former plan’s reading of the race regulations: runners and pacers were expected to carry the required kit throughout. This is an archive, not current race guidance.
